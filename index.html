Icuwan – I Came Up With a Name – is my micro-investment platform, helping brilliant ideas survive and grow. In the future, it will be more than that: a growing investment company where my team and I discover, fund, and support visionary thinkers. Too many great ideas die before they reach the world; Icuwan bridges that gap, turning creativity into real-world impact. Our mission is to empower innovators while building a community of investors who share the vision.

Our Vision

Icuwan exists to fund startups and innovative ideas, taking very little stake—typically below 5% depending on the project. Our principle is simple: we want to help founders grow their vision without taking control. Our target is to support creative thinkers and empower them to bring their ideas to life, while building a community of like-minded investors who share the mission.

By maintaining minimal ownership, we ensure founders retain control and freedom, while we provide the resources and guidance needed to turn concepts into reality.
