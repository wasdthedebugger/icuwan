From the Founder’s Table

What is Icuwan?

Nikas Ghimire

Founder, Icuwan LLC

In season 1 episode 1 of the TV series "Breaking Bad", Walter White tells Jesse Pinkman, "But you know the business and I know the chemistry. I’m thinking maybe you and I could partner up". Icuwan – I Came Up With a Name – is my micro-investment platform, helping brilliant ideas survive and grow. In the future, it will be more than that: a growing investment company where my team and I discover, fund, and support visionary thinkers. Too many great ideas die before they reach the world; Icuwan bridges that gap, turning creativity into real-world impact. Our mission is to empower innovators while building a community of investors who share the vision.


Icuwan is a micro-investment platform designed to discover, support, and help ambitious ideas become real businesses.


The long-term vision is to build an investment company where founders, investors, and builders come together to create meaningful impact.
