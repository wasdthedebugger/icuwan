From the Founder’s Table

What is Icuwan?

Nikas Ghimire

Founder, Icuwan LLC

Icuwan LLC is a holding company and innovation platform built to build, own and support ambitious ideas, startups, and projects. What started as a vision to help brilliant founders and early-stage ideas grow has expanded into something bigger: an umbrella where different ventures, companies, and initiatives can be created, supported, and developed.


Icuwan works across multiple areas, including startup support, technology, investments, and new ventures. Each project under Icuwan represents a different opportunity to build, experiment, and create meaningful impact.


The long-term vision is to build a connected ecosystem of founders, builders, investors, and innovators — bringing different ideas together under one umbrella while creating companies that solve real-world problems.
